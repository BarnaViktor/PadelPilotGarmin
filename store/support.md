# Padel Pilot Support

Padel Pilot currently supports Garmin Forerunner 265 only.

For help, bug reports or feature requests:

- Email: barna.viktor97@gmail.com

When reporting a problem, include the Padel Pilot version, watch software
version, scoring configuration and the steps that led to the issue. Do not
attach FIT files publicly if they contain health or location data. Send such
files by email only after agreeing on a safe troubleshooting method.

Padel Pilot is a free-of-charge app and does not provide an emergency or continuously
monitored support service.
